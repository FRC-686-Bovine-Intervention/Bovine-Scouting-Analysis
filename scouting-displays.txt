This document describes the 2024, 2025, 2026 scouting spreadsheets and how we'd hope to visualize them in the Scouting Analysis app.  Importantly, we'd like to build a framework that can extend this to future games with as little friction as possible.  One question is whether it is worth putting time into teaching the app how to perform these calculations, or whether we should perform the point summations and derived data within a spreadsheet and just have the app read from that data.

The format of our scouting data has changed from year to year.  So another question is whether we can create a flexible framework that is extensible for future games that would make it easier to import into the scouting analysis app (preferrably no changes to the scouting analysis app at all)

Blanks in scouting data indicate the event didn't occur (equivalent of false, no or zero)

Terms:
Displayable metric: a metric that would appear in the drop-down menu for the Team Detail plot, Analysis plot, Sort Builder, Team Detail Comparison, etc.
Badge: badge that appears on team list, pick lists, etc. for that team
